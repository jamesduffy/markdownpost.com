Privacy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

We try to keep very little. This page says what MarkdownPost stores, why, who else touches it and how to get rid of it. We do not sell your data and we do not run advertising or third-party analytics on the site.

What we store

  • Your account: username, a hashed password (we cannot read it), when you joined and last signed in, and an optional email address that stays private (it is used only for email confirmation and password resets).
  • Your profile and site: whatever you choose to fill in, such as name, location, bio, photo, links, site title, tagline, About text, and your theme settings. Profile text and photo are public.
  • Your posts and images: text, titles, drafts, visibility, and uploaded images. Public posts are public; unlisted posts can be read by anyone with the link; private posts and drafts are visible only to you (and to the person who runs the site, who has admin access).
  • "Helpful" marks: which account marked which post, and when. They are private and only influence ordering.
  • Agent tokens: a name you give, the first characters of the token, its permission level and when it was last used. Only a hash of the token is kept, never the token itself.
  • Rate-limit records: to slow down abuse we count attempts (sign-ins, sign-ups, password resets, contact messages, reports and similar) by IP address and, for sign-ins, username. These records are short-lived and are cleaned up automatically.
  • Contact messages: what you type into the contact form (name, reply email and message if you give them, the topic, the page you were writing about) and your username if you were signed in. When the site is set up to do so, a copy is also emailed to the person who runs it.
  • Reports: the post or profile reported, the reason, your note, and who reported it: your account if you were signed in, otherwise only a scrambled (salted hash) form of your IP address that is used to avoid duplicate reports.

Cookies and browser storage

  • Cookies: a session cookie when you are signed in, and a security (CSRF) cookie used by forms. Both are needed for the site to work. We do not set advertising or tracking cookies of our own. Short notices such as "Profile saved" can be carried in a cookie for one page view.
  • Local storage in your browser: your light/dark choice, the Plain view setting, and copies of drafts so the editor works offline. These stay on your device.

  • An email provider, if the site is set up with one, sends confirmation, password-reset and contact-notification emails.
  • The hosting server and its ordinary logs belong to the person who runs the site.

How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, posts and images: until you delete them or your account.
  • Rate-limit records: a short time.
  • Contact messages and reports: as long as we need them to deal with the matter. A report you made stays after you delete your account, but without your name on it.

Deleting your data

Delete your account in Settings, Account. This permanently deletes your profile, your posts (drafts included), your uploaded images and photo, your "helpful" marks and your agent tokens. Public copies made by others, for example by search engines or by people who saved your post, are not ours to delete. To ask about anything else we hold about you, use the contact form and include a reply email.

Contact

Use the contact form. We do not publish an email address.
